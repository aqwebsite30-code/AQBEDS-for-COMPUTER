## I3 — Review engine

One request per delivered order, sent three days after delivery, to the email on the order.
No incentive, no discount, no gift. Never buy, never edit, never delete a genuine review.
Target 50 verified UK reviews; current count is 41. The request template is item 3 of the
post-purchase sequence in `docs/seo-plan.md`, blocked on the verified sending domain.

## I4 — Digital PR

One data-led story per quarter, built only from data this site actually holds: order volume
by region, storage-bed share of orders, average bed size chosen, delivery-date lead times.
No story may be written until the owner approves publishing order data in aggregate.
